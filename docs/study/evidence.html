Where the evidence is not yet complete

  • Hardware counters (ncu: DRAM bytes, occupancy) are blocked on Kaggle: the bandwidth numbers are computed from ideal bytes, not measured bytes.
  • Calibration metrics after full training (ECE/ACE/MCE, reliability diagrams) are the planned paper replication on the RTX PRO 6000 (Sprint 10).
  • The ~15 ms gap between isolated and in-step timing of the compiled original soft loss (page 6) is unexplained.
